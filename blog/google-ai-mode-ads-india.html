Google AI Mode ads in India: what to change now

Short answer: if you run Search, Shopping or Performance Max in India, your ads are already eligible to appear inside Google's AI answers, and you cannot opt out of that separately. The brands that win this shift will be the ones whose feeds, creative and pages give the AI something good to work with.

Parth Agarwal3 Nov 20267 min read

On 28 October 2026, Google announced it is scaling ads in AI Mode, its conversational search experience, to ten more markets. India is one of them. The announcement came from Shashi Thakur, Google's VP and GM for Search Ads, and named Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore.

For Indian brands this is not a new product to sign up for. It is a change to where your existing ads can show. Here is what Google has actually said, and what we would change this quarter.

What did Google announce?

Ads in AI Mode, which started in the US, are now scaling internationally. Google's blog says it plans to add more locales in Q4 2026, including Brazil, Mexico and the UK. It did not give a separate India launch date, and its multi-turn ads, which follow a conversation across follow-up questions, were described as launching in the US. Treat that format as US-only until Google says otherwise.

Which campaigns can show in AI Mode?

Google Ads Help says existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible. No new campaign type and no separate budget is needed. AI Max for Search, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max can match both on direct commercial intent and on the wider context of a conversation. Exact and phrase match only cover direct commercial intent.

The formats are text ads and Shopping ads, labelled Sponsored, shown directly within or alongside the AI response. Google says it is testing local inventory and vehicle ads.

Can you opt out?

Not separately. Google's help page is explicit that you cannot target ads only to AI Overviews or AI Mode, and you cannot opt out of them separately from Search. If you run Search in India, you are in.

There is one more thing you do not control. Shopping ads in AI Mode can carry an AI-generated explainer describing why a product might suit the shopper, and advertisers cannot write, edit or switch off that explainer. Its raw material is your product data.

How is an AI Mode ad different from a classic search ad?

Classic Google search ads compared with ads in AI Mode, based on Google's own documentation.
Dimension Classic search ad Ad in AI Mode
Where it shows Above or below a list of links Within or alongside an AI-written answer
The query Short keywords On average three times longer, per Google
What triggers it Keyword match Commercial intent plus conversation context for broad match, AI Max, Shopping and PMax
Your control over the words Full, you write the ad Partial, Google may add an explainer you cannot edit
Opt out Not applicable Not separately from Search

Why longer questions change your creative

Google says AI Mode queries average three times the length of a traditional search. People stop typing "protein powder" and start typing their whole situation: their budget, their goal, their constraints. Ads and landing pages built around a two-word keyword have little to match against that. Pages and assets that speak to specific needs, use cases and objections have far more.

Performance Max is where this bites hardest, because it assembles ads from whatever assets you hand it. A thin asset group with one video and three images gives Google's systems little to choose from when the question is specific. A deep library of product films, use-case cuts and formats for every placement gives them range.

What Indian brands should change this quarter

  1. Audit your Merchant Center feed. Complete titles, attributes and descriptions are what Google's explainers are built from. Google's help page specifically recommends complete feeds.
  2. Deepen your Performance Max assets. Supply video and image variants for different use cases and buyers, not one hero asset resized.
  3. Use the AI features Google points to. Its guidance lists AI-powered targeting, Smart Bidding, text customisation and final URL expansion.
  4. Rewrite landing pages to answer questions. Lead with direct answers to the questions buyers actually ask, in plain sentences.
  5. Check your geo and language settings. Google's help page lists English as the supported language for these ads, so Hindi and regional-language campaigns should not assume coverage yet.

Paid is only half of the answer

An AI Mode answer has two kinds of real estate: the sponsored slot you can buy, and the sources the answer cites, which you cannot. Being cited is earned the same way across AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude. Engines pull from pages that state a clear answer early, back claims with specific numbers and named sources, use structured comparisons such as tables, and come from brands that independent sites also mention.

That is why this article opens with a short answer, cites Google's own pages, and includes a table. It is also why brands should treat their blog and service pages as citation targets, not just keyword pages. Paid and organic now sit next to each other inside the same answer, and the brands that show up in both look like the obvious choice.

Where creative fits

AI Mode does not make creative matter less. It makes depth matter more, because the system needs enough variety to match very specific questions. That is the problem our AI ad creative track is built for: performance video from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 per video, delivered in five business days, so a Performance Max account can carry real range instead of one resized hero film.
